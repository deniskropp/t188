# How does exposing subconscious cues improve transparency?

Exposing the internal "subconscious" state via `/state` provides:

- **Transparency**: Users can see *why* the AI might be steering the story a certain way. For example, seeing a "Pattern: Impending Doom" explains a shift in tone.
- **Control**: Users can identify misinterpretations—such as the AI focusing on a minor detail as a major cue—before they manifest in the narrative text, allowing for correction before the story is written.
